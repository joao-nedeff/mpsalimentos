# Product

## Platform

web

## Stack

static HTML/CSS — single page, no dependencies, deployed on Cloudflare Pages (custom domain mpsalimentos.com registered at GoDaddy). Chosen for a one-product site: light, fast, trivial to maintain.

## Users

Primary: confectioners and professional buyers (confeitarias, docerias, chocolatiers) who purchase hollow chocolate shells in volume to fill and resell. They evaluate a supplier quickly and reach out through a direct channel (WhatsApp or e-mail) to negotiate an order.

## Product Purpose

Present MPS Alimentos' single product — "Trufas Ocas" (hollow milk-chocolate truffle shells) — and convert an interested professional buyer into a direct contact. Success = the visitor sees the product clearly and messages the company.

## Positioning

Ready-to-fill hollow chocolate shells made with Brazilian ("nacional") milk chocolate, at a consistent standardized size — a wholesale ingredient for confectioners who add their own fillings.

Brand stance (client-provided, 2026-08-13): "Chocolate nacional, feito aqui." A domestic
producer positioned against the imported chocolate that dominates the market — offering
competitive quality without foreign-currency cost, long lead times, or distance. Values:
national production (supply stability), Brazilian cocoa chain, competitive quality vs.
imported peers, and closeness (direct service, no intermediaries, no time zone). Framing:
a new company that turns "just starting" into an advantage — grows by listening to each
client. Voice: direct, confident, plain-spoken, addresses the buyer as "você".

## Operating Context

Buyers reach the site (likely mobile-first, Brazil), assess the product spec, and start a conversation over WhatsApp or e-mail. No online checkout, cart, or catalog — contact-driven sales.

## Product Principles

1. The product is the hero — a professional buyer decides largely on how the chocolate looks; show it appetizingly and let it lead.
2. Make contact effortless and unmistakable; a wholesale buyer should reach WhatsApp/e-mail in one tap.
3. Signal a trustworthy, food-grade supplier through clean, precise presentation — spec stated plainly (size, weight, chocolate type).
4. Keep it light and fast; buyers are often on mobile.
